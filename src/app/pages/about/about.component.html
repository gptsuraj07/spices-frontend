OUR HERITAGE & MISSION

Aridhu — Rare. Pure. Timeless.

In classical Tamil, "Aridhu" (அரிது) means that which is rare, precious, and unrepeatable. We founded Aridhu to rescue traditional South Indian spice pounding from machine-ground commercial mediocrity.

FOUNDER'S VISION

My Goal

I am a working professional who has always loved to cook. With limited time and energy, I discovered that the right flavours and fresh ingredients can make it possible to create delicious, traditional dishes with ease and in much less time.

This experience inspired Aridhu Foods — my goal is to help today’s busy families enjoy authentic home-style food without spending hours in the kitchen.
